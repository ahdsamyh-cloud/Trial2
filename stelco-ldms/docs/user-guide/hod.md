# Head of Legal (HoD)

You see everything, including **restricted** matters, and you have final approval.

* **Dashboards:** open matters by unit and status, SLA breaches, turnaround, workload per lawyer, upcoming hearings and deadlines, litigation exposure, compliance heat map, awareness coverage. Filter by unit, department and dates. Download the **monthly / annual management report** (PDF or Excel) from the dashboard.
* **Approvals:** drafting requests come to you when a Unit Head escalates them, when the Unit Head drafted or reviewed the document (maker-checker), or under an optional rule (Configuration → System settings → `drafting.hodRules`, off by default). Open the request → **HoD approve** or **Return for correction**.
* **SLA escalations** reach you one business day after a breach.
* **Restricted matters:** only people named on them, and you, can see them. Only you can remove a restriction. You're notified when someone restricts a matter.
* **Legal hold and disposal:** you can release legal holds. **Retention** lists records past their retention period; nothing is destroyed unless you choose *Dispose* (password + code required).
* **Access:** you're alerted whenever someone is given HoD, Unit Head or Auditor rights. Review the quarterly **access review** (Users → Access review). For leavers, use the user list → *Reassign* to move their open work.
* **Audit log:** Audit log (password + code). The chain-verification banner must be green.
* **Configuration:** SLA targets, holidays, request types, templates, law firms, courts and regulators.
