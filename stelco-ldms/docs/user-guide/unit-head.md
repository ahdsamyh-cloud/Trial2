# Unit Head

* **Intake:** new requests for your unit appear in the **Intake inbox**. You can convert them only into your own unit.
* **Assigning:** open a matter → **Assign** (Drafting: the *Assign* workflow step). The box shows each person's open work and suggests the least-loaded lawyer. Choose a **reviewer (checker)** who is not the drafter. Bulk-assign from the list by ticking matters.
* **Approving (Drafting):** *Approve* when the final version is uploaded with stage "Final". You can't approve something you drafted or reviewed; it goes to the HoD automatically. To send a request to the HoD yourself, use **Escalate to HoD** with a reason.
* **SLA:** you're told when a matter in your unit breaches its target (warning at 75% to the lawyer, breach to you, HoD one business day later). Business days are Sunday–Thursday, excluding public holidays. *Awaiting information* pauses the clock.
* **Unit dashboard** and **reports** for your unit. Use **Bulk remind** to nudge assignees.
* **Litigation Unit Head:** roll back a register import if needed; close cases after the firm evaluation is recorded.
* **Compliance Unit Head:** review submitted checklists, rate items, verify closure of findings.
* **Awareness Unit Head:** approve the annual plan.
* **Restricting a matter:** *Restrict*, then tick who may see it (assigned people are added automatically). The HoD is notified.
