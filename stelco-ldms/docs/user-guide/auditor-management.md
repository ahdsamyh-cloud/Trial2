# Auditor and Management viewer

## Auditor
* Read-only access to matters (except restricted ones you're not named on), documents, registers, reports and the **audit log**. Your access ends on the date IT set.
* Every page you open and every export is recorded.
* Audit log: filter by person, action, record and date. The banner confirms the hash chain is intact. Downloads are watermarked if that setting is on.

## Management viewer
* **Dashboards → Management overview:** open and closed matters by unit, open SLA breaches, litigation exposure by risk, compliance score, awareness coverage.
* Confidential matters are counted but never described.
* Download the monthly and annual **management report** (PDF/Excel).
