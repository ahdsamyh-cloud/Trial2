# Getting started

## Signing in
1. Open **https://legal.stelco.com.mv** on a computer or phone (office, home or mobile data all work).
2. Enter your username and password.
3. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator).

**First time:** you'll get a temporary password (or a setup link by email). After the password step:
1. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app and enter the code it shows.
2. **Write down the 10 backup codes** and keep them safe. Each works once if you lose your phone.
3. Choose your own password: at least 12 characters. A short sentence works well, for example *"three green boats at Male harbour"*. Common or breached passwords are refused.

## Staying safe
* You're signed out after 15 minutes of inactivity (5 minutes on a phone) and always after 8 hours.
* **My account** shows where you're signed in. Use *Sign out all other sessions* if you've lost a device.
* Sensitive actions (exporting a full file, changing access, viewing the audit log) ask for your password and code again.
* Confidential pages are never stored on your phone. You need a connection to use LDMS.

## Install on your phone
On Android (Chrome) use *Add to Home screen*. On iPhone (Safari) use *Share → Add to Home Screen*. LDMS then opens like an app.

## Notifications
The bell shows new items: assignments, mentions, SLA warnings, reminders for hearings and deadlines. Under **My account → Notifications** choose email (when enabled) and a daily or weekly digest.

## Finding things
* **Search** looks across matters, documents (including scanned PDFs), emails, laws, contracts and opinions. You only ever see what you're allowed to see.
* **Calendar** shows hearings, deadlines, limitation dates, compliance checks, contract and PoA expiries and sessions. *Add to my calendar* downloads an `.ics` file that opens in Outlook.
* Every list has filters, *Columns*, *Save view* and *Export to Excel* (exports are logged).

## Lost phone or locked out?
* After 5 wrong passwords the account locks for 15 minutes.
* Lost phone: sign in with a **backup code**, then go to *My account → Generate new codes*, and ask IT to reset two-factor so you can enrol the new phone.
