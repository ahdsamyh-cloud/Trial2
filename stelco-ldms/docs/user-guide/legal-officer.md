# Legal Officer / Lawyer

**My work** (home page) lists your open matters, nearest deadline first.

## Drafting
1. **Start drafting** once assigned.
2. Need something from the requester? **Request information** (with what you need). The SLA clock pauses until you click *Information received*.
3. Upload your draft (Documents → Upload, stage **Draft**) and **Submit for review**. Your reviewer is notified.
4. If it's returned, fix it, upload a **new version**, and submit again. *Compare last two* shows what changed between Word versions.
5. After review, upload the **Final** version. The Unit Head (or HoD) approves.
6. **Send to requester**: the final version becomes visible to them in the portal. Then **Close**, or **Follow-up required** with a date.

As a **reviewer** you'll see *Mark reviewed* or *Return for correction* (with comments). You can never review your own draft.

## Litigation (cases assigned to you)
* Record each hearing; after it, **Enter summary** (what happened, orders, next steps, actions for the company) within **2 business days**. Entering the next hearing date creates the next hearing and calendar entry.
* Log every update from external counsel (*Log update*) so the case isn't flagged as stale after 30 days.
* Add deadlines and limitation dates (you'll get reminders at 30, 14, 7 and 1 days, and on the day).
* Verify counsel invoices; record settlements and judgments (amounts are stored encrypted).

## Compliance
Maintain laws and obligations, send circulars, review department checklists, rate items, and verify findings are fixed.

## Awareness
Plan and present sessions, upload materials 5 business days before, open the **QR check-in** at the session, and review feedback.

**Comments:** use `@username` to mention a colleague. Tick *Internal note* for anything the requester must not see.
