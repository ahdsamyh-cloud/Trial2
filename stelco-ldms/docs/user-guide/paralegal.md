# Paralegal / Legal Clerk

## Recording a request (Intake inbox → New intake)
1. Received by email? Save the email from Outlook (*File → Save As* → `.msg`) and **drag it onto the form**. Sender, date, subject, text and attachments are filled in for you. Check them.
2. Otherwise choose Letter, Phone or Walk-in and fill in the fields.
3. **Save intake.** You get an intake number (`LGL-INT-…`); attachments are virus-scanned automatically.
4. A request already recorded from the same email is refused, which prevents duplicates.

## Triage
Open the intake and choose one of:
* **Convert to matter**: pick the unit and request type. A reference like `LGL-DRF-2026-0042` is issued. Then **Copy reply** and paste the acknowledgement into your Outlook reply.
* **Link to existing**: for follow-up emails about a matter we already have.
* **Duplicate** or **Not a legal matter** (with a reason).

Register and acknowledge within **1 business day**.

## Other tasks
* Upload court documents, evidence and correspondence to the right matter (choose the document type).
* **Litigation register import:** Litigation → Import register → upload Excel → match columns → **dry run** → fix errors (download *rows to fix*) → Import. Only the Unit Head can roll back.
* Schedule hearings and record attendance for awareness sessions.
