# Department focal point (requester)

* **Ask Legal for help:** *My requests* → **New request**. Describe what you need, attach documents, choose the priority and the date you need it by. You get an intake number at once and a reference (`LGL-…`) when Legal registers it.
* **Follow your department's requests:** status and due date. When Legal sends the result, the final document appears on the request. Reply to Legal with the comment box.
* **Compliance:** *Compliance* shows circulars to acknowledge (**I have read this**), checklists to answer (answer each item and upload evidence, then **Submit to Legal**), and findings to fix (write the action plan, upload evidence of closure).
* **Awareness sessions** for your department, and **Bulletins & FAQ** for common legal questions.

You only see your own department's information. Legal's internal notes are never shown to you.
